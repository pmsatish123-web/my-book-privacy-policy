Privacy Policy for My Book

Effective Date: October 2026 | Application: My Book

Summary: My Book is a digital bookkeeping and cashbook application designed with privacy in mind. Your financial entries, transactions, customer records, and ledger balances are stored locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to any third party.

1. Introduction

Welcome to My Book ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your business records, customer transactions, and ledger entries.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, stored, and managed when you use the My Book Android application.

2. Information We Collect and Store

To provide digital ledger and cashbook capabilities, the App manages the following information:

  • Business Profile Information: Business/Shop Name, Merchant Name, Business Phone Number, Store Address, and UPI ID (for generating your in-app payment QR code).
  • Customer & Supplier Contacts: Name, phone number, address, and notes that you voluntarily input to record credit/debit balances.
  • Financial & Transaction Records: Transaction amounts, credit/debit types (Gave / Got), payment modes (Cash, UPI, Bank), dates, notes, and invoice details you author.

3. Where Your Data is Stored (Local On-Device Storage)

All customer accounts, transaction entries, cashbook logs, and business profile details are stored locally on your device within a secured SQLite database managed by the Android Room Persistence library.

  • Your financial data resides on your physical phone/tablet.
  • We do not transmit your transaction records to any external advertising servers.
  • You retain full ownership and control over all data authored in the App.

4. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

My Book follows the principle of least privilege and requests only standard permissions necessary for core app features:

  • Phone Dialer & Messaging Intents: When you choose to call a customer or send a payment reminder via WhatsApp or SMS, the App opens your device's native phone dialer or messaging app with the customer's phone number and balance summary. The App does not read your private personal messages or call logs.
  • Network State & Internet: Used solely for standard network connectivity checks and platform stability.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App does not sell your customer lists, vendor details, or transaction figures to data brokers, advertisers, or credit bureaus.

6. Data Security

We take the protection of your business data seriously. The App relies on the sandboxed security model of the Android operating system, ensuring that data stored by My Book cannot be accessed by other unauthorized applications on your device.

7. User Control, Data Export & Deletion

You have full authority over your data at any time:

  • Editing: You can edit or modify customer details, transactions, and business profiles directly from the app interface.
  • Deletion: You can delete any individual transaction, customer, supplier, or account book whenever you choose.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Clearing app storage or uninstalling the application from your device will permanently erase all locally stored data.

8. Children's Privacy

My Book is intended for business owners, merchants, freelancers, and adults managing personal finances. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements in our application or changes in regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
